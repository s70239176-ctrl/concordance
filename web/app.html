◆Fragility

⚖Rulings

Where evidence cannot settle a dispute, ask validators one narrow question: does the proposition hold as a matter of what a clause, policy or rule says? Consensus ratifies only HOLDS, DOES NOT HOLD or UNDETERMINED. A ruling settles that proposition; the stored record is never modified.
